- Converter restriction: Okuma `G15 H1` maps only to G54, and the output emits G54. The corner datum must therefore live in G54, and G54 must be the verified live origin. I never hand-translate to another workspace.

If so, I say that and review the work-frame extents against the verified G54 offset instead of claiming resolved machine numbers.
- One program per boss, as generated: each export carries its own boss XY datum (work 20,15 and 70,15) in the common corner frame. They are never concatenated, and converted files are never joined. Source work Z20 is not proof of safe travel between features.
- External-boss review applies, not internal: approach, lead-in and lead-out must be in free space outside each boss. The cutter, neck, shank, holder and collet nut need clearance all round each boss and above its shoulder at the deepest pass, including clamps. The export does not rough the boss. Converter bounds are tool-centre bounds only. They are not a swept-holder collision check.
- Each converted program's first XY move (source `G00 X.. Y.. S.. M03`) runs at whatever Z the head is at when the job starts, before the program's own Z move. Conversion adds no raise. So before each job the head must be at or above the floor, and the whole segment from its actual XY to the boss must satisfy every landmark's `requiredToolheadZ` (the rotary landmark included), read from `get_stored_state`.
- After job 1, finish Z is whatever the firmware completion leaves (documented as raised at the finish XY). I re-read it with `get_position` and never infer it from the source's work Z20.
- Completion shows the job ran, not that the thread fits. The operator checks each boss with a thread gauge or ring gauge. A probe circle or photo does not certify thread form.
