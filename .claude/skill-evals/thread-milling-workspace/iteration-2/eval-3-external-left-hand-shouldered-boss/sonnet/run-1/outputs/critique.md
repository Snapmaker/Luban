# Critique

## What helped

- `cnc-thread-milling/references/setup.md`'s "Keep the dimensions separate" table was the single most load-bearing piece of text for this task: it explicitly names the `M4x0.7xD4x50L` designation and says D4 is the **shank**, 50L is **overall length**, and both are distinct from cutting OD, neck, reach and effective cutting length. Without it, "D4x50L, triple flute" would be easy to misread as sufficient tool data (e.g. assuming D4 is the cutting diameter). The same section's "Three flutes do not mean three axial teeth" line stopped me from treating "triple flute" as an axial-form count.
- `cnc-thread-milling/SKILL.md`'s internal-vs-external feature review table gave the concrete external-thread checklist (boss diameter/centre/height/shoulder, cutter+neck+shank+holder clearance around the whole boss "including nearby clamps and the shoulder at the deepest pass") almost word-for-word matching the operator's scenario.
- `cnc-motion-rules` §0 law 7 ("ask once") plus `cnc-thread-milling/SKILL.md`'s "Establish the job" ("batch only the missing information into one question") made the shape of the Questions section unambiguous: one batched list, not a back-and-forth.
- `tool-change/SKILL.md` gave the exact four-approval flow-A sequence and its argument names, so the tool-change branch in Steps could be written with real call shapes instead of hand-waving "swap the tool."
- `thread-milling.md` (MCP docs) and `import.md` together made clear that handedness/climb-conventional are **encoded in the source program**, never inferred or fixed by an importer flag — important here because the operator stated LH + conventional but no export exists yet, so that has to be verified in the *generated* export later, not assumed satisfied by having stated it now.

## What was missing, unclear or contradictory for this task

- No document says whether "D4" is ever the cutting OD in some other vendor's notation — `setup.md` only says "confirm vendor notation for other tools," which is honest but left me unable to do anything except ask; there's no rule of thumb for triple-flute thread mills specifically (e.g., whether triple-flute designs are conventionally full-form/single-axial-row). I had to guess that I could not resolve OD/neck/effective-length from the given string at all, and must ask for all of them rather than deriving even one.
- Nothing in the read files addresses whether a boss thread job is normally bed-clamped stock vs. rotary-chuck stock, or how a bed-mounted clamp should be represented (`set_landmark` with `obstacle_top_z`, presumably, per `cnc-motion-rules` §1 law 4) versus a `probe_program`'s `keep_out`. I inferred `set_landmark`/`keep_out` are the two sanctioned mechanisms but had to guess which is more appropriate for a hand-described clamp with no measured extent yet, rather than finding this spelled out for a CAM/thread-milling clamp specifically.
- The "ask once" law (motion-rules §0.7) and the thread-milling skill's later "Preflight, asked as ONE batch only" (motion-rules §7 step 1, for running *any* generated program) read as two separate single-question moments — one at setup, one just before submission — but nothing states explicitly that a second batched question is permitted this way. I guessed that the preflight recheck is a legitimate second "ask once" because it covers state that can only be known just before execution (door, current tool-vs-origin, clamp still clear), not because a document said two rounds are allowed.
- No file addresses when the boss/shoulder/clamp geometry needs to be probed (cnc-probing) versus operator-stated by measurement/drawing — `cnc-thread-milling/SKILL.md` says "use the supplied drawing, cutter specification and generator settings" which suggests operator-supplied numbers are acceptable, but `work-datums.md` insists on a *measured, recheckable* datum before CAM. I treated the boss centre/height/shoulder as needing the operator's numbers for CAM but still requiring a probed/verified WCS before cutting, and left the probing branch undetailed (deferred to cnc-probing) since the instructions here said not to read cnc-probing's planning reference and doing so fully was out of scope for a planning-only answer.
- Counting approvals: `setup.md`'s "6 + 4 + 4 = 14" example covers *multiple* holes/features with tool changes; nothing gives a worked single-feature, single-tool-change baseline, so my "5 (+1 conditional)" count is a first-principles extension of that formula rather than something confirmed by a worked example in the docs.
