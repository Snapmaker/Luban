COMPARISON AND RECOMMENDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Four Tabs:     144.0 mm² total retained, 2.0 mm avg ligament
Two Bridges:   220.0 mm² total retained, 5.5 mm avg ligament

RECOMMENDATION: Two C-Shaped Bridges (Variant 2)

RATIONALE:
1. STIFFNESS: Wider ligaments (5–6 mm vs 2 mm) reduce vibration and chatter risk.
2. GRAIN ALIGNMENT: The two side bridges align with the wood grain (Y-direction),
   which is the typical strong direction for wooden enclosures.
3. SAW-CUT SIMPLICITY: Two straight Y-aligned cuts (one per side) are simpler
   and less error-prone than navigating four separate corners.
4. TOOL PATH: Two continuous side cuts vs four separate plunges reduces tool time
   and cycle complexity.
5. LOAD DISTRIBUTION: The bridges span the B0–B180 transition continuously,
   distributing load evenly along the Y span.

Grain direction if Y-aligned (most likely):

  Tabs: Suboptimal; corner tabs may fail along the grain at the step.
